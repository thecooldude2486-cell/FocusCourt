# Focus Court Website

A modern, bold, and family-friendly website for **Focus Court**, a youth basketball club built for young players, parents, and coaches.

The website is designed to promote Focus Court’s basketball programs, introduce the club’s coaches and teams, showcase training opportunities, support merchandise sales, and make it easy for families to contact the club or apply to join.

---

## About Focus Court

**Focus Court** is a youth basketball club focused on developing young players aged approximately **4–14**. The club provides a supportive and competitive environment where children can learn basketball fundamentals, build confidence, improve teamwork, and enjoy the game.

Focus Court offers a range of basketball experiences, including:

- Beginner-friendly basketball programs
- One-on-one skill development
- Team training sessions
- Local competition opportunities
- Tournament and travel game experiences
- Player development pathways
- Club merchandise and community engagement

The club aims to help young athletes grow not only as basketball players, but also as confident, disciplined, and team-oriented individuals.

---

## Website Purpose

This website acts as the official online presence for Focus Court. It is designed to clearly communicate who the club is, what programs it offers, and how interested families can get involved.

The main goals of the website are to:

- Promote Focus Court as a professional and trusted youth basketball club
- Attract new players and families to join the club
- Explain the different training and game opportunities available
- Introduce the club’s coaches, teams, and values
- Provide clear contact and application options
- Showcase merchandise and club identity
- Build a strong community around youth basketball

---

## Target Audience

This website is built for:

- Parents looking for basketball programs for their children
- Young basketball players aged 4–14
- Coaches and trainers interested in the club
- Families interested in local competitions
- Players wanting extra training or skill development
- Community members looking to support Focus Court

The design and content are intended to feel **clear, professional, energetic, trustworthy, and engaging**.

---

## Key Features

The website includes sections for:

### Club Introduction

A clear overview of Focus Court, including the club’s mission, values, and focus on youth basketball development.

### Training Programs

Information about different training options, such as:

- One-on-one training
- Small group training
- Team training
- Beginner skill sessions
- Advanced player development

### Teams and Competitions

Details about teams currently playing in local basketball competitions, including Hills Hornets local competitions and other tournament opportunities.

### Coaches

A section to introduce the coaching team, their experience, and their approach to developing young athletes.

### Merchandise

A merchandise area where visitors can explore or purchase Focus Court branded items.

### Contact and Applications

Clear contact information and application options for parents or players who want to join training sessions, games, or club programs.
